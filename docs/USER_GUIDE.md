# Store Manager — User guide

A simple guide for the shop owner. No computer knowledge needed. If you can
tap a button on your phone, you can run your whole shop with this app.

> **What this app is for**
> You buy goods and hand them to your salespersons *on credit*. They sell to
> people and pay you back over time, then take more. This app remembers every
> item, every handover, and every payment for you — and works out all the
> totals automatically. No more paper register, no more arithmetic, no more
> "did he pay me or not?".
>
> The app needs **no internet**. Everything stays on your phone.

---

## 1. The five things you do (and nothing else)

Everything in the shop is one of these five actions. Learn these and you know
the whole app.

| You do this… | When… | What the app does |
|---|---|---|
| **Stock in** | You buy goods from your supplier | Adds them to your stock, lowers your cash |
| **New sale** | A salesperson takes goods | Lowers stock, adds to what they owe you, makes a receipt |
| **Record payment** | A salesperson gives you money | Lowers what they owe, raises your cash, counts your profit |
| **Return goods** | A salesperson brings goods back unsold | Puts goods back in stock, lowers what they owe |
| **Expense** | You pay rent, transport, wages, a bill | Lowers your cash and your profit |

**One golden rule:** buying goods from your supplier is always **Stock in** —
never an Expense. Expenses are only your *overheads* (rent, electricity, wages,
transport). Keeping them separate is what makes your profit number honest.

---

## 2. The five tabs at the bottom

| Tab | What you'll find |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your shop at a glance: cash, stock value, money owed to you, profit. Quick buttons to do anything. |
| **Products** | Your list of goods. Add new items, add stock, see what's running low. |
| **Sales** | Record a new sale, a payment, or a return. See your recent sales. |
| **People** | Your salespersons, sorted by who owes you the most. Tap one to see their full account. |
| **Reports** | Profit, expenses, who is selling best — for the month, quarter, or all time. Export to a spreadsheet. |

The **gear icon ⚙️** on the Home screen opens **Settings**.

---

## 3. First-time setup (do this once)

1. Open **Settings** (gear icon on Home).
2. Set your **Store name** (e.g. "Malik Store"). It appears on receipts.
3. Set your **Currency symbol** (e.g. `Rs `, `$`, or leave blank).
4. Set **Opening cash** — how much money you have in hand today.
5. Set the **Low-stock alert** number (default 20). When an item drops to this
   many or fewer, the app warns you to reorder.

> **Want to explore first?** In Settings tap **Load demo data** to fill the app
> with a realistic example shop you can play with. When you're ready for real
> use, tap **Clear all data** to start fresh and empty.

## 4. Step-by-step: the everyday tasks

### Add a product (type its name only once)
1. **Products** tab → **Add**.
2. Fill in **Name** (required), and optionally code, size, brand, category.
3. Enter the **Buy price** (what it costs you) and **Sell price** (what the
   salesperson owes you for it).
4. Save. From now on you never type this product's name again — you just pick
   it from a list.

### Stock in (you bought goods)
1. **Home** → **Stock in** (or tap a product → **Add stock**).
2. Pick the product, enter how many you bought and the price you paid.
3. Save. Stock goes up; your cash goes down by what you paid.

### New sale (a salesperson takes goods)
1. **Home** or **Sales** tab → **New sale**.
2. Pick the **salesperson**.
3. Add each item: pick the product (it shows how many are in stock), enter the
   quantity. The price fills in automatically (you can change it). The running
   total updates as you go.
4. The app **won't let you sell more than you have** in stock.
5. Save → a **receipt** appears. You can **share or print** it (WhatsApp, etc.).
   The receipt shows their previous balance, this sale, and their new balance —
   but **never** shows your cost or profit.

### Record a payment (they gave you money)
1. **Home** or **Sales** → **Record payment**.
2. Pick the salesperson (you'll see how much they currently owe).
3. Enter the amount and date. Save.
4. Their balance drops, your cash rises, and your **profit is counted** for the
   share of the payment that is profit. If they pay more than they owe, the app
   warns you first but still allows it (they'll have a credit).

### Return goods (unsold goods come back)
1. **Sales** → **Return goods**.
2. Pick the salesperson and the items coming back. Save.
3. Goods go back into stock; what they owe goes down.

### Record an expense (an overhead)
1. **Home** → **Expense**.
2. Choose a category (Rent, Transport, Wages…), enter the amount and date.
3. Optional: turn on **"Mark as monthly recurring"** for bills you pay every
   month (rent, wages). The app will remind you to add them next month — it
   never adds them silently.
4. Save. Cash and profit for the period go down.

## 5. Reading the Home screen

- **Cash on hand** — actual money you hold right now.
- **Stock value** — what your unsold goods are worth (at cost).
- **Owed to you** — total your salespersons still owe.
- **Profit · Month** — profit earned so far this period. Profit is only counted
  as salespersons actually *pay you*, never before — so it's real money, not
  hope. Use the **Month / Quarter / All time** selector at the top to change
  the period.
- **Low stock · N items** — items at or below your alert level. Tap to see them.
- **Top salespersons** — who has taken the most goods this period. Tap a name
  to open their full account.

Negative cash or profit shows in **red** as a warning.

---

## 6. Fixing a mistake

There is no "edit" button — and that's on purpose, to keep your numbers always
correct. Instead you **delete and re-enter**:

1. Tap the wrong entry (a sale, payment, return, expense, or stock-in).
2. Tap **Delete this entry** and confirm.
3. Add it again correctly.

Because every total is calculated fresh from your records, deleting a mistake
instantly puts every number back exactly as it should be.

---

## 7. Removing people and products (the safety rules)

The app protects your history so your numbers always add up:

- **A product that has been used in any sale or stock-in cannot be deleted.**
  Instead, **Archive** it — it disappears from your lists but its history stays.
- **A salesperson can only be removed once their balance is zero** (they've
  paid for everything). Old receipts and records stay intact.

---

## 8. Protecting your data (very important)

All your data lives **only on this phone**. If the phone is lost, stolen, or
breaks, the data is gone — *unless you have a backup*.

### Make a backup (do this weekly)
**Settings → Back up everything.** This makes one file and lets you send it to
**Google Drive, WhatsApp, or email**. That copy is safe off your phone.

The app reminds you with a banner if you haven't backed up in 7 days. **Don't
ignore it.**

### Restore on a new phone
Install the app on the new phone → **Settings → Restore from backup** → pick
your backup file → confirm. Everything comes back exactly as it was.

---

## 9. Reports & exports

The **Reports** tab shows, for the period you pick:

- Goods taken and returned, money collected, profit, expenses, and **net profit**.
- **Expenses by category** and your recent expenses.
- **Who sells more** — salespersons ranked by goods taken.
- **Export** — download your Products, Salespersons, or Transactions as a
  spreadsheet (CSV) file to open in Excel or Google Sheets.

---

## 10. Quick answers

**Do I need internet?** No. The app works fully offline, in airplane mode.

**Where is my data?** On your phone only. Back it up regularly (Section 8).

**Why doesn't profit jump up when I make a sale?** Because profit is only real
when you're *paid*. It grows as salespersons pay you back, not when they take
goods.

**Can a salesperson owe a negative amount?** Yes — if they pay or return more
than they owe, they'll have a credit. The app warns you first.

**I typed a wrong amount.** Delete that entry and add it again (Section 6).
